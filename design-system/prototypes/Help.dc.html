Working offline

Gisik Nokma keeps working when the network does not. Every change you make is recorded as a claim — a statement about what you did and when — and queued until the server acknowledges it.

A claim in transit is the same claim

In-transit work is drawn with a dashed outline, never a different colour. Recolouring it would say it means something else. It does not: it means the same thing, not yet acknowledged.

Nothing is discarded silently. If two devices recorded different values for one field, the ticket shows you both and waits.

The sync strip at the bottom of every screen names the current state: Live, Offline, or the server’s own reason if it declined a claim.
