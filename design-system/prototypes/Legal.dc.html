What we store

Your email address, the workspaces you belong to, and everything you record in a ticket — its text, its state changes, and the times they happened.

Because this product keeps a history rather than a current value, a change you make is added to a ticket’s record; it does not overwrite what was there. That is the point of the product, and it is also a privacy fact worth stating plainly: editing a field does not erase the earlier value from the history.

We do not sell anything, run advertising, or share your workspace contents with any third party.

Who can see it

Only members of the same workspace. Fields marked restricted are hidden from guests in every client and are excluded from MCP entirely — an assistant connected to your account cannot read them.
