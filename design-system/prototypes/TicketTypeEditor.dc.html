Changing the workflow moves 23 tickets

23 of 41 Field visit tickets sit in a state Field ops v2 does not have.

You can move them to the nearest matching state, or leave them where they are and resolve each one by hand.

Neither choice is recommended over the other — leaving them is a decision, not a deferral.

Implications

  • Two pickers will re-order: the board’s New ticket menu and MCP’s create_ticket.
  • The restricted field stays hidden from guests in every client.
  • Published workflows are versioned — nothing already recorded is rewritten.
