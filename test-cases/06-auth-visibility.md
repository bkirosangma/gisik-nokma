- **6.5** ✅ `proj_memberships` resolves effective roles. A grant records who granted it, when, and at which stream position; a re-grant updates the role in place, one row per membership identity, carrying the later event; a revocation removes the workspace row so the role stops being conferred, and touches only the revoked user; project roster events add and remove project-scope rows carrying the project-admin flag, and a project removal leaves the workspace row and other projects standing.
