## 28.1 Domain (T1)

<!-- drafts T1 -->

- ✅ **28.001 — `ticket.created` accepts `draft: true` only with `start: 'backlog'` and no `stateId`:** it is refused with `start: 'focus'`, with `start` absent, with a `stateId`, and with `draft: false`; the refusal message is `a draft is born in the backlog: send start: "backlog" and no stateId`. Control: the same payload without `draft` still parses (a v1 event), with `start: 'focus'` and with `start` absent. Mutation: drop the second `.refine` → the `start: 'focus'` arm parses.
- ✅ **28.002 — `ticket.published` is strict `{projectId: ULID}` and `ticket.discarded` is strict `{}`;** both registered at version 1 against entity kind `ticket` only, each once in `registrations()`. Control: `parseEvent` accepts both on a ticket and refuses both on a note with `entity-kind-mismatch`. Mutation: drop `.strict()` from `ticketPublishedPayload` → the extra-member arm parses.
- ✅ **28.003 — the reducer: a draft creation sets `draft = {authorId, at}` from the envelope (actor and server time), stays in Backlog with `rank: null`; `ticket.published` removes the key and re-pins `workflowId` / `workflowVersion` to the type's CURRENT binding while the state stays Backlog;** control: a ticket whose type was re-pointed while it was a draft ends on the NEW workflow, and a ticket created without `draft` has no `draft` key (`'draft' in state === false`) and is unchanged by the same type re-point. Mutation: skip the re-pin → the new workflow id is not reached.
- ✅ **28.004 — `ticket.discarded` sets `draft.discarded = true` and keeps `authorId` and `at`; `ticket.published` and `ticket.discarded` on a ticket that is not a draft throw, naming the ticket;** control: both fold on a draft, and `isDraft` / `isDiscardedDraft` read the flag (`isDraft` stays true after a discard so every door can tell "gone" from "published").
- ✅ **28.005 — THE CENSUS (server) now covers the draft pair:** both types are in `TICKET_EVENT_TYPES` (so the census of 27.004 stays empty), `payloadIds('ticket.published', {projectId})` names the project, `payloadIds('ticket.discarded', {})` names nothing, and a committed `ticket.discarded` notifies nobody (owner ruling 2026-10-04: a discard leaves no trace). Control: the planted type is still reported. Mutation: remove `'ticket.published'` from `TICKET_EVENT_TYPES` → red naming it.
- ✅ **28.006 — History narrates the pair:** a `ticket.created` with `draft: true` reads `Drafted as <Type>`, `ticket.published` reads `Published`, and `ticket.discarded` reads `Discarded` (never shown: the stream is erased, but the census of 3.183 wants every type narrated). Control: a `ticket.created` without `draft` still reads `Created as <Type>`. Mutation: ignore `draft` → the first arm reads `Created as`.
- ✅ **28.007 — the author's allowlist:** `draftAllows` is true for exactly the eight allowlisted types and the exempt bookkeeping types, and every other ticket-scoped registration is refused with `A draft cannot be <verb>. Publish it first, or discard it.` (`ticket.deleted`: `A draft cannot be deleted. Discard it instead.`). The CENSUS partitions the registry's ticket-scoped types into allowed, exempt and refused, so a new ticket type must pick a side; control: a planted `ticket.planted_for_census` is reported as unclassified. Mutation: add `ticket.transitioned` to `DRAFT_ALLOWED_EVENT_TYPES` → red naming it.
- ✅ **28.008 — the live-ticket gate refuses both on a deleted ticket** (`deleted-ticket`) and the E6-2 table covers the new registrations (so a ticket-scoped type that picks neither side fails the case). Control: the same events pass the gate on a live twin.
- ✅ **28.009 — `TICKET_EVENT_TYPES` has nineteen members and both draft types are in it;** control: the registry check in the same `it` still holds for every member. (`packages/domain/test/ticket-state.test.ts`)

Controls: a 500, a 409 whose body is another rule, a 409 `draft.publish-refused` with no `cause`, and a 422 carrying the `draft.publish-refused` body are rethrown as the `HttpError` they are.
- ❌ **28.136 — `discard_draft` previews first:** without `confirm_name`, with a wrong-case name and with a name with a trailing space, the answer is the `Preview` (`Erases the draft "<title>", N sub-drafts and M attachments uploaded to them (permanently deleted). Linked notes are kept. This cannot be undone.`, counts `{ subDrafts, attachments }`, `nothingChanged: true`, `confirm: confirm_name must be "<title>"`) and no event is sent. With the exact title it sends one `ticket.discarded {}` for the draft (actor the session user, origin `mcp`, a `parseEvent`-valid envelope) and answers `{ ticketId, discarded: true }`, making no read of the drafts list.
- ❌ **28.137 — `discard_draft` on a ticket that is not a draft:** refused `This ticket is not a draft. Use delete_ticket to remove a published ticket.`, even with the exact `confirm_name`, with no event sent and no read of the drafts list; an unreadable ticket is `ticket not found or not visible to you`. Control: the same call on a draft previews.
- ❌ **28.138 — the erasure set:** `eraseSetOf` counts every descendant of the draft (children and grandchildren) and the attachments of the draft and of those descendants, and nothing from a sibling, a sibling's child or another root; it terminates on a `parentId` cycle; an id the list does not hold counts zero.
- ✅ **28.139 — `get_ticket` on a draft:** the answer carries top-level `draft: true` and `key: null` besides the server's `ticketState.draft`; a published ticket's answer has no `draft` member.
- ✅ **28.140 — `delete_ticket` on a draft:** refused `refused by draft.not-allowed: A draft cannot be deleted. Discard it instead.` before any preview, with or without `confirm_name`, and nothing is sent. Control: a published ticket still previews with its key.
- ❌ **28.141 — the registry:** `draftTools` is exactly `list_my_drafts`, `publish_draft`, `discard_draft`, each with its input keys and its description pinned word for word; `allTools` ends `list_deleted`, `list_my_drafts`, `publish_draft`, `discard_draft`; the pinned list is 69 tools (14.555) and the stdout sweep (14.88) drives all three.
- ❌ **28.142 — a draft's life through the hosted door:** `create_ticket {draft: true}` for a parent and a sub-draft returns no key; `list_my_drafts` lists both with the project's name; `get_ticket` says `draft: true`; `publish_draft` gives the parent `DRRP-1` and then the child `DRRP-2` (workspace prefix `DR` + project prefix `RP`); the list shrinks as they publish; both `ticket.created` rows and both `ticket.published` rows are origin `mcp`, actor the caller. A third draft with a sub-draft previews `1 sub-drafts and 0 attachments`, is discarded on its exact title, and within a moment `get_ticket` answers not found and the list no longer holds it.
- ❌ **28.143 — nobody else's assistant:** a draft made by a plain member is invisible to the workspace owner's assistant: `list_my_drafts` is empty, `get_ticket` and `discard_draft` answer `ticket not found or not visible to you`, `publish_draft` answers `refused by permission.visibility: This ticket is not visible to you.` (the same words for a random id); the draft is still the author's afterwards (`get_ticket` says `draft: true`, the list holds it).
- ❌ **28.144 — a refused publish passes through and changes nothing:** publishing a sub-draft while its parent is still a draft throws `refused by draft.parent-unpublished: ` and the server's sentence; both drafts are still listed; once the parent is published the same call succeeds.
- ❌ **28.145 — the other tools on a draft, through the real server:** `update_ticket` (a new title) and `assign_ticket` (`me`) work on the author's draft; `add_comment` is refused `refused by draft.not-allowed: A draft cannot be ...Publish it first, or discard it.`; `delete_ticket` is refused with the literal sentence `A draft cannot be deleted. Discard it instead.`, the same words G1 sends (the early refusal is not a second wording).
