Owner 2026-10-02: "The switches are not aligned." Settings → Notifications is `/account`'s Notifications card; on a phone a two-line label dropped its switch below the text at the left while one-line rows kept it at the right. Block 25.360–25.369.

- ✅ **25.360 — a notification row is one line of layout: `.gn-account__notification-row` sets `flex-wrap: nowrap` and `justify-content: space-between` AFTER the shared four-selector base rule that wraps;** control (same `it`): the session, workspace and passkey rows still wrap and no rule narrows them to `nowrap`, and the base keeps `align-items: center`. Mutation: drop `flex-wrap: nowrap` → red. `apps/web/test/notification-switch-align.test.ts`.
- ✅ **25.361 — the label takes the column and wraps inside it, the switch is `flex: none`:** `.gn-account__notification-row .gn-switch__label` is `flex: 1 1 auto`, the shared label carries `min-width: 0`, `.gn-switch` is `flex: none`; control: the shared `.gn-switch__label` has no `flex` and `.gn-switch-row` no `justify-content` or `flex-wrap`, so Settings → Workspace's switch (control beside its own label) is unchanged. Mutation: drop the label's `flex` → red. `apps/web/test/notification-switch-align.test.ts`.
- 🧪 **25.362 — browser frame (the worktree on a lane port, signed in, `/account`) at 390, 360 and 430, neo dark/light and hig light: every row's switch right edge is the row's right edge (±1px), whatever the label's line count, and the switch is centred on its row.** Before: right edges 346 and 95 at 390 (spread 251px), 316 and 95 at 360, 386 and 95 at 430. After: spread 0.0 in all four frames, centre offset 0.0. Evidence in the DONE message of `task/smoothness-g4-g5-phone-fixes`.

## 25.37 Ticket-sheet field bands are one rounded band on a phone (G-5)

Owner 2026-10-02: "The rows which have a lighter bg, they seem chopped off." (ticket sheet, 390, neo dark). Cause, measured: the tinted row is a two-column grid on desktop (`dt` the left cell with left corners rounded, `dd` the right cell with right corners rounded); the phone arm stacks them, so the label strip was square on its right, the control block square on its left, the control sat flush to the band's left edge while its label sat 12px in, and the full-screen sheet's own `:focus-visible` outline ran down both screen edges beside the body. Block 25.370–25.379.

- ✅ **25.370 — on a phone the tinted row is ONE band: the phone arm gives `.gn-ticket-sheet__field--tinted` the `fill-quaternary` background and the full card-inner radius, and makes its `dt` and `dd` transparent with radius 0, after the base cell rules;** control (same `it`): desktop keeps `dt` rounded left, `dd` rounded right and filled, and no base rule styles the row itself. Mutation: drop the phone row rule → red. `packages/ui/test/ticket-sheet-bands-phone.test.ts`.
- ✅ **25.371 — the control shares the label's inset:** the phone arm gives the tinted `dd` `padding-left: 0.75rem` (the label's inset); control: the base `dd` keeps `padding: 0.75rem 0.75rem 0.75rem 0` and the plain (untinted) `dd` gets no phone left padding. Mutation: drop it → red. `packages/ui/test/ticket-sheet-bands-phone.test.ts`.
- ✅ **25.372 — the full-screen sheet draws no focus ring along the screen edges:** `.gn-sheet:focus-visible { outline: none }` lives in the `max-width: 64rem` arm only (the panel is a `tabindex=-1` focus container, not a control); control: the close button keeps `2px solid var(--gn-focus-ring)` and nothing removes the ring above 64rem. Mutation: drop it → red. `packages/ui/test/ticket-sheet-bands-phone.test.ts`.
- 🧪 **25.373 — browser frame (the worktree on a lane port, signed in, the seeded ticket) at 360, 390 and 430, neo dark/light, hig light, minimal dark: each tinted row is one box (the row's rect equals the union of its `dt` and `dd`, both transparent and square), its left and right insets inside the fields well are equal (8px / 8px), it lies inside the sheet body's content box, its corners all carry the radius (12px neo, 8px hig/minimal), the label and control share a left edge, and the sheet's outline is `none` while it holds focus.** Before (390 neo dark): the Assignee `dt` `[32, 509.7, 358, 536.5]` with radius `12px 0 0 12px`, its `dd` square on the left and flush.
